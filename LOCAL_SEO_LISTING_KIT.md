Cleanings and Routine Exams:

Same-Day Dental Crowns:
CEREC same-day crowns designed, milled, and placed in one visit whenever appropriate, without a two-week temporary crown.

Dental Implants:
Permanent tooth replacement planned with modern imaging to restore chewing function, appearance, and long-term stability.

Invisalign:
Clear aligner treatment for adults and teens who want to straighten teeth without traditional braces.

Emergency Dental Care:
Same-day help when available for tooth pain, broken teeth, chips, swelling, lost crowns, and urgent dental concerns.

Porcelain Veneers:
Cosmetic veneers designed to improve tooth shape, color, spacing, and smile balance with a natural look.

Teeth Whitening:
Professional whitening options for brighter teeth with guidance to reduce sensitivity.

Sedation Dentistry:
Relaxation options for anxious patients or longer dental visits, based on medical history and treatment needs.

Root Canal Treatment:
Modern root canal therapy to relieve infection-related tooth pain and help save the natural tooth when possible.
